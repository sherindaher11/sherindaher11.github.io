Technical Documentation Infrastructure & Strategy Consultant

Helping engineering organizations design, build, and scale modern documentation systems.

I work on the systems behind documentation: strategy, architecture, docs-as-code infrastructure, contribution models, governance, developer content, and AI-ready workflows.

What I Help With

Documentation problems are often system problems, not writing problems.

I help teams determine what documentation they need, how it should be structured, where it should live, how contributors should work with it, and how the system should evolve as the product and engineering organization scale.
